direction, field). Unknown direction is explicit absence of directional evidence,

All numeric domains are raw. Scale and physical unit are Unknown unless stated.

or sender identity. OP18..1B bit 0 defaults to read, with unknown high bits
preserved independently on each side. Opaque data extends through end-of-data:
minimum zero and maximum unset. Empty data is a representation case, not
empty-packet acceptance. No radio transport, crypto engine, pairing or native
scan is established.
